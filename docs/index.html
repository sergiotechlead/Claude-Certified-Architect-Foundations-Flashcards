    60 practice questions in the proportions of the Claude Certified Architect (Foundations) exam guide, version 1.0: 60 questions, 120 minutes, pass mark 720 on a scale of 100 to 1,000. The guide gives a weight for each of the 5 domains and lists 30 task statements without weights, so the count per domain is rounded from its weight (16, 11, 12, 12 and 9), with at least one question per task statement. Each question is set in one of the six exam scenarios; the exam presents four of them. Five questions ask for two answers, as multiple-response items do. Unofficial study aid. Every question was written for this page from the public exam guide, and none is a real exam item. Advice from a candidate who passed: start with the official Anthropic courses and their hands-on exercises with the Claude API, then practice the question format with mock exams such as claudecertificationguide.com. The real scenarios are longer than the ones here. Created by Sergio Beleño. Take the exam runs the 60 questions as one timed simulation; its score on the 100 to 1,000 scale is an estimate, because the official conversion is not published.
